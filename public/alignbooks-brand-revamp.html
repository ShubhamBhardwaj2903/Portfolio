01 — Context

What AlignBooks is,
and where it stood.

AlignBooks is India's leading ERP and cloud accounting software for MSMEs — covering everything from GST billing and inventory to payroll, HR, POS, and production management. It serves thousands of businesses across retail, manufacturing, pharmacy, hospitality, and more.

Despite the product's depth and market position, the website and brand identity hadn't kept pace. The visual language felt dated — mismatched typography, an inconsistent colour palette, a cluttered navigation structure, and no seasonal or campaign-oriented design presence.

For a software brand competing with funded players like Zoho and Tally, first impressions matter enormously. The website was the primary acquisition channel — and it wasn't doing the product justice.

02 — Brand Audit

What the old brand
was getting wrong.

Before touching a single pixel, I audited the existing site (test.alignbooks.com) against the new design (alignbooks.com) to identify what was working and what wasn't.

The old site had the skeleton of a good product website but suffered from a lack of visual intentionality — everything from typography choices to colour usage felt arbitrary rather than designed.

  • Heavy serif and system fonts made headlines feel corporate and stiff — poor for an aspirational SaaS brand
  • The blue-dominant palette was safe and expected — it failed to differentiate AlignBooks from dozens of identical ERP competitors
  • Navigation was dense and unstructured — users reported difficulty finding features, pricing, and modules
  • The hero section led with a product name instead of a user benefit — no clear value proposition above the fold
  • No consistent design system — spacing, card styles, button shapes, and section layouts were inconsistent across pages
  • Zero campaign or seasonal design presence — the brand went dark during peak Indian festive shopping seasons

06 — Landing Pages

Designed to convert,
not just to impress.

AlignBooks runs active paid and organic marketing campaigns targeting MSMEs across India. The old landing pages were generic — the same homepage layout repurposed for every campaign, with no tailoring to intent, audience, or offer.

I redesigned the landing page system from scratch — focused on conversion principles: a single strong headline, a benefit-first value proposition, social proof, and a low-friction lead capture form above the fold.

The result was a set of modular, campaign-ready templates that the marketing team could deploy quickly across industries, features, and seasonal promotions — each with consistent brand language but tailored messaging.

  • Single-column, distraction-free layout — no global nav, no exit paths except the CTA
  • Benefit-led headline above the fold — problem → solution → social proof within the first scroll
  • Integrated lead capture form with minimal fields — name, phone, email — reducing friction
  • Industry-specific variants — the same template adapted for pharma, retail, manufacturing, CA/CPA
  • Festive offer integration — landing pages could carry seasonal themes tied to homepage campaigns
  • Mobile-first layout — the majority of AlignBooks' marketing traffic comes from mobile users

08
08 — Reflections

What I
took away.

01
Brand Is a System, Not a Style
A rebrand only works when it's a coherent system — type, colour, spacing, motion, and tone all pulling in the same direction. Changing just the colours or fonts in isolation creates a patchwork, not a brand. Building the system first made every subsequent design decision faster and more consistent.
02
Festive Design Is a Strategic Asset
India's festive calendar is one of the most commercially significant in the world. A brand that shows up — that feels alive during Diwali, Holi, and Independence Day — builds emotional connection and trust that generic campaigns can't buy. Building a seasonal design system was one of the highest-leverage things I did.
03
Navigation Is UX and Business Strategy
Restructuring the nav wasn't just a design improvement — it was a business decision. When users can find what they're looking for (features, pricing, industries), they stay longer and convert better. The mega-nav with grouped categories directly contributed to the bounce rate improvement.
04
Conversion Design Is a Discipline
Landing pages for lead generation require a completely different design mode than informational pages. Every element — headline, CTA placement, form length, visual hierarchy — needs to earn its place. Applying conversion principles to the landing page system had a measurable downstream impact on campaign performance.
